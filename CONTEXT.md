# Uplink Knowledge Base

The Uplink Knowledge Base explains Uplink's offerings and working model to the public audiences around the IT freelancer network.

## Language

**Knowledge Base**:
A public documentation site for Uplink's freelancers, clients, recruiters, and agencies.
_Avoid_: Docs, help center, handbook

**Locale**:
A language-specific version of the Knowledge Base.
_Avoid_: Translation, language copy

## Relationships

- The **Knowledge Base** has one or more **Locales**
- The German **Locale** is the root and default version
- The English **Locale** is available under `/en/`

## Example dialogue

> **Dev:** "Should the English **Locale** replace the German **Locale** at the root?"
> **Domain expert:** "No — the German **Locale** stays the default, and English remains under `/en/`."

## Flagged ambiguities

- "site" was used broadly during the migration; resolved: use **Knowledge Base** for the public documentation product and **Locale** for a language-specific content tree.
